Privacy notice.

How Prime Energies collects, uses and protects personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

01Who we are

Prime Energies (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party for the personal information described in this notice. Our Information Officer handles all privacy questions and requests.

02What we collect

  • When you contact us through our website form, email, phone or WhatsApp: your name, company, work email, phone number, area of interest and your message.
  • When we do business together: business contact details of people at client and supplier organisations, and the information needed for proposals, contracts, invoicing and supplier registration.
  • Client operational data: when a client engages us, we process the data it provides (for example fleet, fuel or metering data) as an operator on the client’s behalf, under a written agreement and the client’s instructions.
  • Our website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting and font providers receive your IP address and browser details, which are technically needed to deliver the pages.

03Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange meetings and prepare proposals.
  • To deliver our services and manage contracts, invoices and support.
  • To meet our legal, tax, B-BBEE and procurement obligations.
  • To send relevant business updates, only where the law allows it, and you can opt out at any time.

We do not sell personal information.

04Do you have to give it?

No. Sharing your information with us is voluntary. Without it, we may not be able to respond to your enquiry or provide our services.

05Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host our email, files and website, such as Google Workspace, GitHub and Cloudflare, under confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Professional advisers, such as our auditors and legal advisers, where needed.
  • Regulators and authorities, where the law requires it.

We never share one client’s data with another client.

06Transfers outside South Africa

Some of our service providers store information outside South Africa. We only use providers bound by data protection terms that give a level of protection comparable to POPIA, as section 72 requires.

07How we protect it

We use access controls, two-step verification, encryption in transit and least-privilege access. If personal information is compromised, we will notify the people affected and the Information Regulator, as section 22 of POPIA requires.

08How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as the law requires, for example tax records. After that we delete it or de-identify it. Enquiries that do not lead to further engagement are deleted when they are no longer needed.

09Your rights

  • Ask whether we hold information about you, and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed.
  • Object to processing, including direct marketing, and withdraw consent you have given.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Send requests to privacy@primeenergies.co.za. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

10Direct marketing

We only send marketing messages to people who have agreed to receive them, or to clients about services similar to those they already use. Every message tells you how to opt out, and we honour opt-outs promptly.

11The Information Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information, you can contact the Information Regulator (South Africa):

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za
  • General enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za
  • POPIA complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
  • Phone: 010 023 5200
